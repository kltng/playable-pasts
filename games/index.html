What a listing does and does not promise

Checked: the file

Self-contained, nothing fetched during play, no AI needed to run it, no secret keys, a Sources & assumptions screen, real buttons. Re-checked automatically whenever the gallery is rebuilt.

Reported: the human tests

Whether a person opened it offline, played it by keyboard, and timed it. Shown as the contributor reported it, including what they left untested.

Not checked: the history

Nobody here vets whether a game's argument is sound. That is why the history bible ships with every game — so you can judge it the way you would judge a reading.

Not checked: your classroom

Your network, your LMS, your projector, your students. Download the file and open it once on the real setup before class.
